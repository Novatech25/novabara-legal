Community guidelines

Version française

NovaBara supports respectful professional exchanges. These rules apply to profiles, photos, listings, services, reviews and messages.

  • No exploitation or sexualisation of minors, pornography or sexually explicit content.
  • No threats, incitement to violence, promotion of terrorism, hate, discrimination or harassment.
  • No scams, impersonation, deceptive listings, illegal activity or prohibited goods and services.
  • No spam, manipulated reviews or unauthorised disclosure of personal data. Respect intellectual property.
  • Only publish information and images you have the right to use and that honestly describe your offering.

Use Report on profiles, content or messages. Block a user in a conversation or in settings. Our team reviews reports and may remove content or suspend an account. A limited text filter supplements human review; it does not detect every abuse or analyse images.

Reported message evidence is limited to that message, retained for up to 30 days for review and then erased. Our team can review it if the author deletes the message. Deleting a participant’s account erases evidence involving that account.

To appeal a decision or report danger: modernsoftware058@gmail.com.
