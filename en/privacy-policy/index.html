Version française

Operator and contact

NovaBara is operated by NovaTech. For support or personal data requests contact modernsoftware058@gmail.com. This policy covers professional profiles, messaging, appointments, jobs and vehicle listings.

Data and purposes

We process email and authentication identifiers for accounts; names, roles, cities, phone numbers, descriptions, services, prices and photos for profiles; listings, applications, messages, appointments, reviews, favourites and preferences for the features you use. Reports, blocks, moderation decisions and limited technical logs support safety. Passwords are managed by Supabase Auth and are not accessible to support.

Visibility and communications

Approved public profiles, services, reviews and approved listings are visible to service users. Professional contact details may be disclosed through authorised contact features. Messages and appointments are accessible to participants; reported messages are also reviewed by moderators. Messaging is not end-to-end encrypted. Avoid sharing sensitive information. Photos and profile changes are reviewed before publication.

Optional permissions

Location is requested when you use a map or share location for an appointment; background location is not provided. Camera access lets you take a photo and the system picker lets you select photos to upload. Optional notifications use a device token linked to your account. You may refuse or revoke permissions in device settings; related features may be limited. We do not request contacts or microphone access.

Service providers and subscriptions

Supabase supplies authentication, database and storage. Expo, Apple APNs and Google FCM deliver notifications. Maps use platform mapping services, including Google Maps on Android, which receive data needed to display them. Apple or Google process in-app payments; RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous account identifier, products, receipts and subscription entitlements to verify professional access and supply subscription history and analytics. NovaTech does not receive your bank card number. When you choose calls, SMS or WhatsApp, the contact details and text you send are processed by the external service you open.

Hosting and protection

Providers may process information outside your country. Consider their processing locations and terms before sharing information. Access depends on account and role, production connections use HTTPS and unapproved media remain private. No computer system guarantees zero risk. We do not integrate targeted advertising or cross-app advertising tracking and do not sell your data.

Retention

Account data and useful content remain while the account is active or until deleted. Account deletion removes associated data from the active database; files and the RevenueCat customer are queued for deletion with retries. The operational target is completion within 30 days; contact support if a failure delays processing. Reported message snapshots expire after 30 days, closed reports after 30 days, old profile revisions after 30 days (the latest and the active public version remain while the account exists), media review reasons after 30 days and administrative logs after 90 days. Migration archives expire within 30 days and are purged on account deletion. Offline backups must expire within 30 days and deletions must be reapplied before a restore. Apple and Google may retain their own transaction records under their obligations.

Requests and deletion

Edit your profile or delete your account under Settings > Delete my account. You can also request deletion, access, correction or a copy of your data at modernsoftware058@gmail.com from the account email; proportionate identity verification may be necessary. Never send passwords or bank details. Deletion is irreversible. It does not automatically cancel an Apple or Google subscription: cancel in store settings to prevent renewal. Cancellation is not required for immediate account deletion. Rights and remedies under your country’s laws remain applicable.

Updates
